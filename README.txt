ESSEX MOTO WEBSITE STARTER
==========================

Files:
- index.html: website structure and copy
- styles.css: responsive blue-and-black styling
- script.js: mobile menu, category filters and enquiry form behaviour

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a web browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Add a verified Essex Moto business email address in script.js (businessEmail).
- Add the confirmed business phone number and any social links if wanted.
- Replace the stylised category artwork with Storm-approved product photographs.
- Add individual Storm product listings only after confirming the current retail price,
  dropship delivery charge, trade cost and stock status for each item.
- Connect a real form handler or business email before relying on online enquiries.
- Add privacy, terms, delivery, returns and consumer information appropriate to the
  business before taking orders or payments.

CATALOGUE SCOPE
Included: kids' and adult quad bikes, electric and petrol dirt bikes, pit bikes,
balance bikes, helmets/protection, clothing and compatible spare parts.
Excluded: buggies, go-karts and their parts.

PRICING RULE
Use Storm's advertised selling price and applicable customer delivery charge as agreed.
Do not add an extra markup. Confirm trade cost and stock before accepting an order.
No payment checkout is enabled in this starter build.
